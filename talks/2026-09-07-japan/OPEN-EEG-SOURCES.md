# Braindecode, OpenEEG-Bench, and CuttingGardens

Checked September 6, 2026. These are distinct projects; no NTX ownership or formal partnership inferred.

## Braindecode

[Schirrmeister et al. (2017), Deep learning with convolutional neural networks for EEG decoding and visualization](https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.23730), Human Brain Mapping, is the foundational paper. [Current documentation](https://braindecode.org/stable/) identifies the 2017 first release and describes PyTorch, MNE, MOABB, pretrained checkpoints, and Hugging Face integration. Avoid transient model counts or calling historical results current benchmarks.

## OpenEEG-Bench

[Repository](https://github.com/braindecode/OpenEEGBench), [paper PDF](https://github.com/braindecode/OpenEEGBench/blob/main/2026_02_EUSIPCO_benchmark_FM.pdf), [software DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19698863).

Guetschel, Aristimunha, Truong, Kokate, Tangermann, and Delorme, *Toward OpenEEG-Bench: A Live Community-Driven Benchmark for EEG Foundation Models*. Repository citation identifies EUSIPCO 2026. Downloaded and read the paper's abstract/introduction. It builds on experience organizing the 2025 EEG Foundation Model Challenge and proposes shared preprocessing, fine-tuning, open datasets, and community governance. Initial paper results: five models, ten datasets. Current repository scope: twelve datasets, frozen/ridge probes, parameter-efficient methods including LoRA, and full fine-tuning. Distinguish current software scope from paper results; no leaderboard supremacy asserted.
