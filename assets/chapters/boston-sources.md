# Boston chapter: event records, Neurable, and images

Checked 6 September 2026. Selected milestones, not an exhaustive chapter history.

## Chapter and events
- Shivani Sheopory Hernandez lists Boston Chapter Co-Founder, January 2016: https://www.linkedin.com/in/shivanisheopory . This is a participant's self-reported organizing history, not proof of a sole founder or exact first event.
- Official chapter archive: https://neurotechx.com/chapter/1543/ describes scientific/networking events, workshops, and Hacknights.
- An August 26, 2018 interview includes a caption identifying Yannick Roy speaking at a Boston chapter event: https://neumo.jp/en/170107/ . The photograph's date is not established, and its original image URLs returned 404, so it is not used.
- February 5, 2022 London–Boston Buzz-in-Review: see the existing London sources in `chapter-histories-sources.md`. Boston's archived posts corroborate the shared series: https://ww.twstalker.com/NeuroTechBOS . Distinguish the 2022 event from the research year it reviewed, 2021.
- June 29, 2022 neuroethics panel at MIT Media Lab: chapter posts archived at the above URL advertise June 29; NTX's official recap confirms Boston/BrainMind hosting, MIT Press and Dana Foundation support, Adam Molnar's participation, and Alex Higuera's organizing: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/neurotechx_neuroethics-innovation-neurotech-activity-6949844486340427776-FeEb . Attendee account: https://www.yasmeenbrain.com/media . Recording linked there: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wAA9PfhCzmE . Full recording not watched.
- January 2023 Buzz-in-Review: Boston's archived posts describe the Boston–Zurich session and January 17/24/31 global series. This is a separate event from the photographed neuroethics discussion.

## Neurable and the founders
- Adam Molnar's company retrospective, June 8, 2020: https://www.neurable.com/blog-posts/were-neurable-nice-to-meet-you . Describes Ramses Alcaide's 2015 Michigan research roots and the 2017 VR demonstration, followed by a shift toward everyday EEG interfaces.
- U-M Spring 2023 feature identifies both as co-founders and locates the photograph at Boston HQ: https://lsa.umich.edu/lsa/news-events/lsa-magazine/lsa-magazine-archive/Spring-2023/unlocking-the-mind.html . No product efficacy claims from this feature are imported into the deck.
- A separate Neurable-hosted online career event includes both founders: https://luma.com/so-you-want-to-BCI . It is not labeled an NTX Boston event; its date was not available in the retrieved page.
- No claim that either founder established the Boston chapter, or that NTX founded/incubated Neurable. The verified chapter link is Adam's panel participation; Morgan may add firsthand context.
