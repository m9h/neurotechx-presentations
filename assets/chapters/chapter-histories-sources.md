# Toronto, New York, London, and India histories

Researched September 6, 2026. Four slides inserted after the original slide 7. These are selected documented milestones, not exhaustive histories. The community-building lessons are editorial synthesis of those activities.

## Toronto

- February 2016 NTX Digest, by Melanie Segado, republished by OpenBCI: https://openbci.com/community/neurotechx-digest-discover-what-the-neurotechx-community-has-been-up-to/ . Describes January 21, 2016 as the second winter lecture-series meetup, sponsored by Paytm Labs, about EEG signal processing. Its 2015 retrospective names Toronto among the chapters. No exact founding date asserted.
- Historical chapter page: https://neurotechx.com/chapter/1531/ . Describes biweekly Hacknights, EEG 101, Neurodoro, Dharma Defender, and local industry connections. This is historical evidence, not a current meeting schedule or app availability guarantee.
- EEG 101 archive: https://github.com/NeuroTechX/eeg-101 . Began in 2016; archived April 13, 2021. Maintainer describes withdrawal after Muse SDK support was rescinded. Do not present it as a currently available Android app.
- Official student recap: https://neurotechx.github.io/studentclubs/news/student-clubs-2016/ . NeuroTechUofT's VitReous was an EOG eye-gesture system for VR/AR, not EEG-based eye control.
- Asset `toronto-student-demo-2016.jpg`: https://neurotechx.github.io/studentclubs/images/DemoDay2016_NeuroTechUofT.jpg . Original collage labeled Student Clubs — Demo Day 2016: NeuroTechUofT. Do not relabel it a city-chapter lecture or imply its physical venue was Toronto. No individual identities inferred.

## New York

- Founder's account: https://conorrussomanno.com/ . Lists NeuroTechNYC founder/organizer from July 2015, monthly human-computer interface Hacknights, and June 8, 2016 talk on democratizing neurotechnology.
- Creator's project account: https://mathuramg.com/projects/darkMaze.html . Credits Mathura with Jordan Frand, Christina Choi, and Wangshu Sun; explicitly describes testing at a NeuroTechNYC meetup. Inputs combined blinks/EMG, accelerometer head motion, and EEG-related features. Do not endorse the page's alpha/focus interpretation or call all inputs brain signals.
- Video: https://vimeo.com/157092570 . Public oEmbed metadata confirms title `openBCI`, creator Mathura, and description identifying the Neurotech Meetup NYC test. Uploaded February 29, 2016; event date not independently established.
- Asset `new-york-darkmaze-2016.jpg`: the original video thumbnail, https://i.vimeocdn.com/video/558276073-6b8675a5ae9e56c88120e874216c87b38389b3d8a88fa37f34cd0741b95aa3a5-d_640?region=us . Shows laptop with prototype and EEG traces, not a group portrait. Credited explicitly as a video thumbnail.
- Original event URL: https://www.meetup.com/NeuroTechNYC/events/249012309/ . Retrieved public page data identifies `NeuroTechNYC Hack Night - Using Neural Nets to make & classify EEG`, April 6, 2018, 18:30 New York time. URL now redirects to `/neurotechnology/events/249012309/`; do not infer today's group is an NTX chapter. RSVP totals are not attendance counts and are not used.

## London

- Original listing: https://www.meetup.com/NeuroTechLDN/events/230512143/ . Retrieved public page data confirms May 6, 2016, 19:00, LSE New Academic Building, first meetup with NERRI, hosts Javier Asensio and Imre Bard, and three-minute flash talks accessible to non-academics. Agenda included researchers, startups, Cybathlon, and London Hackspace Brain Hackers. No formal incorporation date inferred.
- Organizer announcement: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ben-woodington_neurotechx-ldn-bos-buzz-in-review-2022-activity-6894574014426398720-_Ash . February 5, 2022 hybrid London–Boston Buzz-in-Review; London's venue Bellavita Academy. This is an announcement, not an audited attendance report.
- Organizer recap: https://iclneurotech.co.uk/hackathon . January 25–26, 2025; explicitly credits NeuroTechX among partners and Sophie Valentine among judges. Omit first-ever superlatives and conflicting duration descriptions.
- Asset `london-hackathon-2025.jpg`: organizer gallery `_DSC3849.JPG`, https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/594170b0-f0f2-410a-b1ba-d99158952c22/_DSC3849.JPG/:/rs=w:1200,cg:true,m .
