# Public neurotechnology: exhibit and performance image candidates

Researched and downloaded September 6, 2026 for Morgan's requested slide on interactive displays, dance, and music. These are two separate collaborations, not a documented shared project or institutional lineage. The Exploratorium exhibit must not be attributed to the NSF IUCRC BRAIN Center. Neither source establishes a formal NeuroTechX partnership with the BRAIN Center.

## Cognitive Technology Group / Exploratorium, 2015

Primary project-builder account: Tomás Vega, MindKinetics (February 2015), https://www.tomasvega.com/ . Describes a co-adaptive motor-imagery BCI with one degree of freedom, a robotic arm, OpenBCI, and a dry-electrode headset for the Cognitive Technology exhibit.

Participating project announcement: EyeWire, January 28, 2015, https://blog.eyewire.org/eyewire-at-the-exploratorium/ . Credits m0xy and Cognitive Technologies Group and describes EEG games, VR brain exploration, and EyeWire neurons.

Photo source: Nathan Hurst, Make, *Control This Robot Arm — With Your Brain*, February 2, 2015.
https://makezine.com/article/technology/arduino/control-a-robot-arm-with-your-brain/

- `exploratorium-jisoo-kim-2015-hurst.jpg`: Jisoo Kim at the display, identified by the article. **Photo: Nathan Hurst.** https://cdn.makezine.com/uploads/2015/01/dsc_1291.jpg

Additional contemporaneous account: Sarah Hillenbrand, Berkeley Science Review, February 6, 2015, https://berkeleysciencereview.com/article/2015/02/06/catch-the-brainwave-cognitive-technologies-at-the-exploratorium . Its inline legacy photo URLs return 404; the Make photos above survive. Do not claim the exhibit was clinically validated or provided unrestricted thought-based control.

## BRAIN Center / music, dance, and visualizations

- `meeting-of-minds-lynn-lane.jpg`: stage-wide image with dancers, musicians, and projected visualization. **Photo: Lynn Lane.** University of Houston's *The Neural Basis of Human Creativity*, by Laurie Fickman: https://stories.uh.edu/2024-Pepe-Contreras-Vidal-Brain-Dance/
- Original image: https://stories.uh.edu/2024-Pepe-Contreras-Vidal-Brain-Dance/assets/0s8SHCZO8B/hobby-center-noblemotion-and-musiqa-photographer-lynn-lane-178-4096x2731.jpg
- Rice's project descriptions: https://music.rice.edu/music-mind-and-body-lab-projects
- Center: https://www.nsfbrain.org/

Rice lists **Meeting of Minds (2024 onward)** with NobleMotion Dance, Musiqa, multimedia artist Badie Khaleghian, and the University of Houston BRAIN Center directed by Jose-Luis Contreras-Vidal. Dancers wore mobile EEG equipment; music was composed by Anthony Brandt, choreography by Andy and Dionne Noble, and projections visualized brain-data relationships. Describe it as performance and research, not mind reading or a therapeutic efficacy demonstration.

Further examples from Rice, if Morgan wants a dedicated arts slide:
- **LiveWire (2022):** dance, music, mobile EEG, and live data visualizations.
- **Diabelli 200 (2023 onward):** pianist and conductor wore mobile EEG, with neural-data-influenced projections.
- **Window into the Creative Mind (2024 onward):** pianist Chelsea deSouza, EEG, and research on improvisation.
