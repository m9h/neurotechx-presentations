- Yannick's announcement in the EEGLAB mailing-list archive: https://sccn.ucsd.edu/pipermail/eeglablist/2020/014884.html
- Organizer press release, Novela Neurotech: https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/505553

- Local PDF `neureka-scoring-2020.pdf`; first-page rendering `neureka-scoring-first-page.png`.
