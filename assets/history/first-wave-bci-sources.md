# First wave of BCI: consumer access and community development

Morgan's requested framing: consumer headsets and the promise of controlling your world helped drive community formation. “First wave” is a community-history lens, not the origin of BCI research. The slide separates early entry points from the expanding ecosystem, without claiming all products launched together or caused NTX's founding.
