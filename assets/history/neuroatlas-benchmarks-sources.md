## NeuroAtlas

https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14698 and https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14698v1

May 2026 preprint by Kontras and colleagues at KU Leuven and MIT. Frozen-encoder evaluation is not full fine-tuning. Supervised-pretrained neural baselines are not synonymous with classical non-neural methods. `neuroatlas-2026.pdf` is the original paper; `neuroatlas-first-page.png` is its rendered first page. Paper identifies CC BY 4.0.

## Classical-method comparison

https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/f0f39b7686634fc81ca0112566b2c05f-Abstract-Conference.html

Yang, Sun, Li, Van Hulle, ICLR 2026, Are EEG Foundation Models Worth It? Comparative Evaluation with Traditional Decoders in Diverse BCI Tasks. Code: https://github.com/LiuyinYang1101/STEEGFormer . `eeg-fms-worth-it-page.png` is a genuine official proceedings-page screenshot. Do not conflate compact neural networks with classical non-neural decoders or claim a universal winner. Original third-party rights retained.

## OpenEEG-Bench

https://github.com/braindecode/OpenEEGBench

Current repository includes frozen, parameter-efficient and full adaptation strategies. Initial study scope differs from evolving code. `openeegbench-repository.png` is a genuine repository screenshot, not a results chart. No new leaderboard ranking asserted. Original third-party rights retained.
